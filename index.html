    how it works

    sosreport + journalctl + sysstat → alert
    01

    ingest

    Pull sysstat/node_exporter metrics and journalctl logs from every host, once a minute.

    02

    engineer

    Turn raw readings into 15-min rolling mean, std-dev and trend slope — failures show up as trends, not spikes.

    03

    train

    IsolationForest runs with zero labels from day one. GradientBoosting sharpens as real incidents accumulate.

    04

    score & alert

    Every window gets a failure probability and a plain-English reason: "disk at 98%, filling up."

    05

    enrich (bonus)

    Cross-reference alerts against Red Hat Insights rule hits for vendor-confirmed context.
